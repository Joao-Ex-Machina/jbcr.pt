## On AI technologically

As of the writting of this post (summer 2026), I still think there is **nothing** that is inherintly **ground-breaking** in current AI.

For those not familiar, the architecture of AI is based on matrix multiplications (MatMul) and activation functions, so basic algebra.
Of course you can argue that the the way we organize such MatMuls is the true science behind AI, however I would disagree...

The true ground-breaking science on AI, for me, is the **Backpropagation** algorithm, which ables the model to "learn" (in a layman's sense) and update its matrixes. The idea for this algorithm exists since the XVII century, but has been **roughly frozen since the 80's**.

But if this algorithm is so ancient.

**The driving on AI as never been software, it was always the hardware.**
